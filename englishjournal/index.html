Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I get started with English Journal?

A: Simply download the app from the App Store, create an account, and start your first journal entry. We'll guide you through the process with helpful prompts and tips.

Q: Is my data secure?

A: Yes, we take your privacy seriously. All your journal entries and personal data are securely stored and encrypted. We never share your information with third parties.

Q: Can I use English Journal offline?

A: Yes, you can write journal entries offline. Your entries will be synced when you reconnect to the internet.

Q: How do I delete my account?

A: You can delete your account at any time by going to Settings > Account > Delete Account. Alternatively, you can contact us at takayuki.meilyn@gmail.com and we'll process your request within 48 hours.

Q: Is there a subscription fee?

A: English Journal offers both free and premium features. Basic journaling is free, while premium features like advanced grammar checking and personalized learning plans are available through a subscription.

Q: How can I report a bug or suggest a feature?

A: We love hearing from our users! Please email us at takayuki.meilyn@gmail.com with your bug reports or feature suggestions. Your feedback helps us improve English Journal for everyone.

Technical Support

Experiencing technical issues? Here are some common solutions:

  • App not loading: Try restarting the app or your device
  • Sync issues: Check your internet connection and try again
  • Login problems: Ensure you're using the correct email and password
  • Missing entries: Your data is safely stored; try logging out and back in

If these solutions don't help, please contact us at takayuki.meilyn@gmail.com with details about your issue, and we'll assist you as soon as possible.
